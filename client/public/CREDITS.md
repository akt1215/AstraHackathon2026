- Character pixel silhouettes, appearance-editor interaction, layered synthesized sound fallback, and contextual guidance adapted from shresthkapoor7/tilth at d40bf097728a8a1f6f04b18d4486f664f9f123ce (volcanic.js, content/characters.js, engine/onboarding.js, sound.js, engine/quest-guidance.js).
